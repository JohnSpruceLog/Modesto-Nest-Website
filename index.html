About MONESTO!

Digital NEST is a free tech and career center for youth 14–24, offering free training and a path to paid work opportunities in fields like software development, information technology (IT), UI/UX design, and marketing. Along the way, you'll get mentorship, career guidance, and networking opportunities, plus free access to MacBooks and PCs, snacks, games, and a community that sticks with you. No experience needed. No cost required (thank you to our generous donors!).
